## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a trustworthy, serene, and modern boutique hospitality aesthetic specifically tailored for regional discovery in Uzbekistan. It synthesizes corporate reliability with warm hospitality charm, steering clear of hyper-commercial aggregator noise in favor of curated architectural clarity.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** Evoked through deep, grounding Mediterranean-to-Central Asian navy blues and precise typographic structures.
- **Warm & Aspirational:** Conveyed with warm ivory canvas foundations, subtle amber highlights reminiscent of historic brick and sunlit courtyards, and tranquil ocean teal accents.
- **Tactile & Uncluttered:** Smooth generous radii (14–20px), soft diffused elevations, and strict 12-column architectural balance evoke the feeling of entering a tranquil boutique hotel lobby.

### Cultural & Technical Grounding
- **Language & Typography:** First-class typographic fidelity for Uzbek Latin script, ensuring critical characters (`o'`, `g'`, `sh`, `ch`) maintain pristine baseline alignment and diacritic balance.
- **Architectural Architecture:** Formulated explicitly for server-rendered Django MVT architectures using standard Bootstrap 5 container rules, progressive DOM enhancement, and semantic HTML5 states without reliance on SPA-only runtime paradigms.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of verified luxury and structural clarity. The interface relies on a warm, off-white foundation (`#F8FAFC`) rather than cold digital white, using pure white (`#FFFFFF`) strictly as elevated surface containers (cards, modals, sticky summary modules).

### Role Allocations
- **Primary (`#102A43`):** Anchor for navigation headers, structural frames, key titles, interactive hero buttons, and high-emphasis labels.
- **Secondary (`#0E7490`):** Interactive focal points, filter toggles, active tab borders, link highlights, and secondary utility badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F4B860`):** Curated accents including star ratings, boutique feature tags, VIP perks, and subtle highlight indicators. Never overused on body backgrounds.
- **Neutral (`#627D98`):** Balanced slate neutral for secondary metadata, room amenities, inactive icons, table headers, and disabled states.

### Status System (Semantic Signals)
- **Success (`#10B981`):** Verified hotel badges, confirmed reservations (`Tasdiqlangan`), positive toast notifications, and completed actions.
- **Error (`#EF4444`):** Real-time booking conflicts (`Tanlangan sanalarda xona band`), form validation alerts, cancellation alerts (`Bekor qilingan`), and destructive actions.
- **Warning/Pending (`#F59E0B` / `amber-soft`):** Explicitly used for pending reservation states (`Kutilmoqda`), payment-on-arrival warnings, and session notices.

## Typography

The type scale integrates geometric personality with high-density editorial readability.

### Type Pairing
- **Headings & Price Displays:** **Manrope** provides contemporary warmth, architectural geometry, and open apertures that prevent optical fatigue in headings and numerical booking breakdowns.
- **Body & Controls:** **Inter** handles data tables, filter options, form inputs, and transactional small print with unmatched legibility and strict baseline metrics.

### Linguistic Guidelines for Uzbek Latin
- Ensure full support for the single quote / modifier apostrophe character (`ʻ` or `'`) used in Uzbek consonants and vowels (`o'zbek`, `g'alaba`, `bog'lanish`, `to'lov`). Font files must never clip diacritics or render apostrophes with disproportionate tracking.
- Numeric quantities and local currency must follow standard spacing: `450 000 so'm` (non-breaking space between thousands and before the suffix).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a standard 12-column responsive fluid grid aligned with Bootstrap 5 container specifications to ensure seamless translation into Django base templates.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Widths
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1320px`, gutters `1.5rem` (`24px`), horizontal canvas padding `2rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 12-column fluid grid, gutters `1rem` (`16px`), horizontal canvas padding `1.5rem`. Search bars and filter sidebars collapse to offcanvas or horizontal wrap.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column fluid layout, gutters `0.75rem` (`12px`), horizontal canvas padding `1rem` (`16px`). Fixed sticky search actions and stacked multi-step flows.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Component Padding:** Standard cards use `space-lg` (`24px`) on desktop, reducing to `space-md` (`16px`) on mobile.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major vertical separations use `space-3xl` (`72px`) on desktop and `space-2xl` (`48px`) on mobile to maintain an airy boutique feel.
- **Form Controls:** Compact `space-sm` (`12px`) vertical and `space-md` (`16px`) horizontal padding within inputs and select dropdowns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ambient diffusion rather than harsh drop shadows, capturing a soft, sunlit depth that feels high-end and unobtrusive.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` (Warm Ivory). Provides the calm background substrate.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White). Applied to cards, hotel catalog rows, and forms. Bound with a subtle structural hairline border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating & Hover Elements):** Elevated cards, dropdown menus, and date-picker overlays use `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Overlay Dialogues & Modals):** Sticky booking widgets and system modals use `box-shadow: 0 25px 50px -12px rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.18)` over a backdrop tint of `rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.45)`.

### Border & Outline Architecture
Never rely on drop shadows alone for accessibility. All interactive cards, inputs, and tab containers feature a 1px border (`#E2E8F0` or `#CBD5E1`) to guarantee visual edge definition on all display calibratons.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates generous, organic curvature reflecting comfort, leisure, and upscale modern hospitality architecture.

### Radius Assignments
- **Small Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) to `0.75rem` (`12px`).
- **Cards & Content Blocks (Hotel cards, Room modules, Summary boxes):** `1rem` (`16px`) up to `1.25rem` (`20px`). This fulfills the target 14–20px rounded aesthetic.
- **Pills & Chips (Status badges, rating indicators, amenities):** `9999px` (Full pill shape).
- **Hero Image Banners & Modal Dialogs:** `1.25rem` (`20px`) to `1.5rem` (`24px`).

## Components

Standardized UI components optimized for server-side HTML/CSS rendering with Django templates and Bootstrap 5 utilities.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button (`btn-primary`):** Background `#102A43`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `12px`, padding `12px 24px`, font weight 600. Hover: `#0A1D30` with subtle elevation. Focus: outline `3px solid rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.4)`. Example: `Bron qilish`, `Qidirish`, `Bronni tasdiqlash`.
- **Secondary Button (`btn-secondary`):** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #0E7490`, text `#0E7490`, border-radius `12px`. Hover: background `#E0F2FE`. Example: `Batafsil`, `Filtrlarni tozalash`.
- **Destructive Button (`btn-danger`):** Soft red background (`#FEE2E2`), text `#EF4444`, hover background `#FCA5A5`. Used for `Bekor qilish` and room removal in management portals.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Status: Kutilmoqda (Pending):** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`, pill radius.
- **Status: Tasdiqlangan (Confirmed):** Background `#D1FAE5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`, pill radius.
- **Status: Bekor qilingan (Cancelled):** Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`, pill radius.
- **Status: Yakunlangan (Completed):** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, pill radius.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` border, `10px` border-radius, standard height `48px`.
- Active / Focus: border `#0E7490` with box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.15)`.
- Error state: border `#EF4444`, feedback caption in `Inter 12px` font `#EF4444` (`"Iltimos, ushbu maydonni to'ldiring"`).

### Hotel & Room Cards
- **Catalog Card (Horizontal on desktop, stacked on mobile):**
  - Left/Top: High-resolution image (16:10 aspect ratio) with subtle badge overlay (`Toshkent`, `4.8 ★`).
  - Middle: Hotel title, address link, amenity pills (`Bepul Wi-Fi`, `Nonushta kiritilgan`, `Basseyn`).
  - Right: Pricing block (`450 000 so'm / kecha`), total estimate, and primary CTA `Batafsil`.
- **Room Card (Detail Page):**
  - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`, rounded `16px`, background `#FFFFFF`. Includes capacity indicators, bed specs, non-refundable/refundable tags, and distinct `Bron qilish` button.
  - Fully-booked state: Greyscale overlay opacity `0.6` with explicit label `"Tanlangan sanalarda xona band"`.

### Notifications & Django Messages
- Rendered as top-level alerts with icons:
  - Success: `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, green check icon (`"Broningiz muvaffaqiyatli qabul qilindi!"`).
  - Error: `#FEF2F2` background, `#991B1B` text, red warning icon.
